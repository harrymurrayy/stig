The Stig Foundation website — a charity built in memory of Jack Murray, the user's brother's best friend, who passed away from suicide.

**Why:** A tribute and ongoing charity initiative in Jack's name.

**Deployment:** Live at www.thestigfoundation.com, deployed via GitHub and Azure.

**How to apply:** Keep the cause in mind when making design, copy, or feature suggestions — the tone should be respectful and sensitive given the subject matter (suicide/mental health).
